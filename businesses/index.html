Every unanswered question costs someone a journey.

"Do you have a room tonight?" "Is breakfast included?" "Is the hall free on the 14th?" Guests ask these questions every day, often by phone, and often after they've already set off. When the answer is hard to find, some people turn up and are disappointed. Others choose a place they could check, even if they would have preferred yours.

Making your information easy to find isn't marketing. It's part of looking after your customers.

Availability is only useful when it's right. Rescue Business is where your team runs the day, so each check-in, reservation and check-out keeps your availability in step with the rooms you actually have. If a guest's signal is too weak to confirm it, Rescue says so rather than guessing.
