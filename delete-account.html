Delete your RESCUE account

Guests: follow the steps below. Business owners and staff: see Rescue Business.

For guests who book rooms, tables, events or order food through the RESCUE app. Deleting your account removes your sign-in and your personal details from our systems. This cannot be undone.

Option 1 — in the app (fastest)

  1. Open RESCUE and sign in with Google if you are not already.
  2. Tap the More menu (top right) and choose Delete my account.
  3. Read the summary, tick the confirmation box and tap Permanently delete.
  4. Confirm with Google when asked. Your account is deleted straight away and the app signs you out.

If you have a confirmed, paid or in-progress booking or order, the app lists it and asks you to finish or cancel it first (normal cancellation rules of that lodge or restaurant apply). Unpaid requests that are still open are cancelled for you.

Option 2 — by email (no app needed)

Email support@rescueapps.com from the Google email address you used to sign in, with the subject Delete my RESCUE account. We check that the request comes from that address, then delete the account and reply to confirm, within 30 days (usually much sooner). If we cannot delete it yet because of an active booking, we tell you which one.

What happens to your data

DeletedYour sign-in (Google account link, email, display name and photo held by our authentication service); your notification (push) tokens; your request-limit counters.
CancelledOpen requests that you have not paid for: pending room or event requests, quote requests, unpaid table bookings and unpaid orders.
Anonymised and kept by the lodgeBooking and order records the lodge needs for its accounts and history (dates, items, amounts and status). Your name, phone number, email, notes, special requests and payment references are removed and replaced with “Deleted guest”, and the record is no longer linked to you.
KeptIf a lodge blocked you, or you were flagged for abuse, we keep that record against your anonymous internal ID only (no name, phone or email), so that deleting an account cannot be used to avoid a block. A short deletion log (internal ID, date, counts — no personal details) is also kept as proof the deletion happened.

Some free-text lines in a lodge's own staff activity log may still mention a name typed by staff. Ask us in your email and we will have it removed.

Your device keeps nothing after deletion: the app clears its saved drafts, baskets and caches.

Rescue Business: owners and staff

Owners. In the Rescue Business app, open the dashboard menu and choose Delete account. You must first close any property that is still active. Deleting your account removes your sign-in (email and password) and your owner profile.

Closed properties and their business records (such as staff, reservations, shifts and sales) are kept so they can be recovered and so guests keep their booking history. To have your business data erased as well, email support@rescueapps.com from your account's email address. We erase it and confirm within 30 days.

Staff. Staff accounts belong to the business. Ask your owner to remove you from the team: this deletes your PIN and signs you out. Your name may remain on past records such as shifts and sales. For anything else, email us.
