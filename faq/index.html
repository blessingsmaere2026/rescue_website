FAQ

Questions, answered plainly.

For lodge owners and for travellers. If your question isn't here, ask us.

For lodge owners

Who can join Rescue Business?

Places to stay: lodges, hotels, guest houses, resorts, motels, apartments, camping sites and game reserves, in Malawi and other countries. Rescue for standalone restaurants, bars and event venues is coming.

How do guests see my availability?

Your rooms show as available or full in the Rescue app. When you check guests in and out and take reservations in Rescue Business, the availability guests see stays in step with your rooms.

Can a walk-in take a room I've already reserved?

Reservations can be marked Reserved or Held, so a room that's already reserved isn't given away at the front desk.

Does Rescue take payments for me?

No. Guests pay your business directly, following your instructions, by the methods you offer, such as mobile money or bank transfer. Rescue doesn't hold guest money.

Can my staff use it?

Yes. Staff sign in with their own PIN, and each role sees what it needs.

How much does it cost outside Malawi?

Rescue Standard is USD 25 and Rescue Pro is USD 40 per month, paid in US dollars. Message us and we will help you set up your property. See Pricing.

What's the difference between Standard and Pro?

Both make your rooms visible and help you manage them. Pro adds your menu and event hall bookings, quotes and payment records. See Pricing.

How do I join?

Message us on WhatsApp on +265 995 197 544 or email support@rescueapps.com.

For travellers

Is Rescue on Google Play?

Not yet. Rescue is in testing and coming to Google Play on Android. Message us for early access.

Does it cost anything?

No. The Rescue app is free for travellers.

If a lodge shows rooms available, is my room guaranteed?

No. Availability helps you decide where to go. Your booking is confirmed by the lodge, so follow its booking instructions.

How do I pay?

You pay the lodge directly, following its payment instructions, by the methods it supports. Rescue doesn't take your money.

What does “Couldn't verify” mean?

Rescue couldn't confirm the lodge's availability, usually because of a weak signal. Rescue tells you that rather than guessing.

Do I need an account?

You can browse without one. You need a Google sign-in to make a booking.

Will it use much data?

No. Rescue is mostly text, with few images.

Is there an iPhone app?

Not yet. Rescue is on Android only for now.

How do I delete my account?

See Delete account.
