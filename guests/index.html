For travellers

Know before you go. Don't travel blindly.

Check places to stay, whether they have rooms, their prices and what's included, before you set off.

Get early accessCheck first, then travel →

You shouldn't have to drive around to find a room.

You reach town after a long journey. The first lodge is full, and so is the next. Each stop costs time, fuel and patience, and by the time you find a room it's late and you've stopped choosing.

Rescue lets you find out before you leave.

Check first, then travel.

  1. 1

    Find a place

    Browse lodges, hotels, guest houses and other places to stay. You don't need an account to look.

  2. 2

    Know what to expect

    See rooms, prices, breakfast and opening hours, plus the menu and event hall where a lodge offers them.

  3. 3

    Book and pay the lodge

    Sign in with Google and send a booking. Then follow the lodge's booking and payment instructions and pay it directly, by the methods it supports, such as mobile money or bank transfer. Rescue doesn't take your money.

    Choose your country code when you enter your phone number. Rescue remembers it for next time.

  4. 4

    Enjoy your stay

    Use My stay with your stay code for check-out reminders, self check-out and breakfast requests.

An honest answer, even when it's "full".

A lodge showing "Full" tonight is still useful. It saves you a wasted trip and points you somewhere else.

When your signal is too weak for Rescue to confirm a lodge's availability, it says Couldn't verify rather than guessing. You always know how sure the answer is.

Seeing rooms available isn't a confirmed reservation. Your booking is confirmed by the lodge.
