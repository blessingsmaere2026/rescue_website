It happens every evening.

A traveller arrives in town after a long day on the road. They stop at the first lodge, and it's full. The second is full too. By the third it's dark, they've used fuel they didn't plan to use, and they'll take any room that's left.

None of those lodges did anything wrong. There was simply no way for the traveller to know.

An illustrative scenario

The same trip, two different evenings.

3 stops · about an hour

Without checking first

  1. 6:00 pmArrives in town, tired after the drive
  2. 6:10 pmFirst lodge: fully booked
  3. 6:30 pmSecond lodge: fully booked, and the receptionist suggests somewhere across town
  4. 7:05 pmThird lodge: a room at last, after an hour of driving around

Remembers the evening as stressful, and remembers none of the lodges for the right reasons.

1 stop · straight there

Checking before leaving

  1. Before setting offChecks lodges in town on their phone
  2. Same minuteSees two lodges are full tonight and one has rooms
  3. Before the driveSubmits a booking, then pays the lodge as its booking instructions say
  4. Once confirmedDrives straight there and checks in

Remembers the lodge that had a room, and the two that were honest about being full.

Same town. Same lodges. The only difference is that the guest knew before the journey.

Every guest deserves an answer before the journey.

Sharing your availability isn't only about filling rooms. It's about respecting people's time.

When a guest can see that you're full tonight, you've still helped them. You've saved them the drive, the fuel and the frustration of turning up for nothing. They can make a better decision, and they'll remember that your information was honest.

A clear "fully booked" is better service than a wasted trip.

Be the lodge they check first.

Many travellers plan where to stay before they reach town. When guests can check your rooms before they travel, your lodge can become part of their plans, rather than somewhere they come across by chance on arrival.

Visible, accurate availability gives you that chance. The choice is still theirs, but you're in it from the start.
