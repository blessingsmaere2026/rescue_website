Rescue Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 November 2026

Who we are

This policy covers two apps operated by RescueApps, Malawi:

  • Rescue (com.rescue.app), for guests and travellers. It shows room availability and the status of bars, restaurants and halls at places to stay, and lets guests make bookings, orders and quote requests.
  • Rescue Business (com.rescue.rescue_business), for owners and staff of lodges, hotels and other places to stay, to manage rooms, reservations, menus, halls, payments and their team.

Rescue is used by guests and businesses in Malawi and in other countries, including Zambia, Zimbabwe, Mozambique, Tanzania, Uganda, Rwanda and Botswana.

Contact for privacy questions: support@rescueapps.com

Using Rescue without an account

You can browse places to stay, rooms, prices, menus and opening status in the Rescue app without signing in.

Information we collect from guests (Rescue app)

  • Google account details – when you sign in with Google (needed to book, reserve a table, order or see My Bookings), we receive your name, email address, profile photo link and an account ID.
  • Details you type – your name, phone number (stored in international format with your country code), notes, special requests, booking and order details, event details for quote requests, and any payment reference or bank name you enter after paying a business directly.
  • Room (stay) codes – if you enter a code from a lodge, we use it to open a short session tied to that one stay so you can request breakfast or food. The session ends at checkout.
  • Notification token – if you allow notifications, we store a device token so we can send booking and order updates. It is removed when you sign out.
  • Device and performance information – Google Firebase services (App Check, Play Integrity, Cloud Messaging and Performance Monitoring) process device identifiers and app performance data to protect the service from abuse and keep it fast. Your IP address is used briefly for rate limiting.
  • Anonymous view counts – we record that a listing was viewed, without any information about who viewed it.

Information that stays on your phone (Rescue app)

  • Location – used only when you tap “Near me”, only on your phone, to sort places by distance. We do not send or store your location.
  • Camera – used only to scan QR codes. No pictures are saved or sent.
  • SIM country – the country of your SIM card is read on your phone only, to preselect your dialling code when you enter a phone number. It is not sent to us. No special permission is needed.
  • Your saved phone number – after your first booking, your phone number is saved on your phone in encrypted storage so it can be filled in for you next time. You can change or remove it under Profile → My phone number. It is cleared when you sign out or delete your account.
  • Drafts and baskets – saved on your phone in encrypted storage, and cleared automatically after a short time or when you sign out.

Information we collect from businesses (Rescue Business app)

  • Owner account – your name, email address and phone number, and a password that is handled by Google Firebase Authentication (we never see it). Password-reset emails are sent through Firebase.
  • Business details – your property's name, contact and WhatsApp numbers, location (map coordinates), country, currency, time zone, rooms, prices, menu, halls and photos you upload. These are shown publicly to guests in the Rescue app.
  • Payment account details – the mobile money and bank details you add so guests can pay you (account name, account number, bank name, branch and, for international transfers, SWIFT code), and optional payment instructions. These are shown to guests who book with you.
  • Staff – for each staff member: name, roles and whether they are active. Staff sign in with a PIN, which we store only in a securely hashed form. Staff are not asked for an email address or phone number.
  • Business activity records – reservations, check-ins and check-outs, shifts, sales, tabs, stock counts, payments recorded against bookings, and an activity log of staff actions (which may include staff names).
  • Subscription records – your plan, billing currency, the amounts, dates, method and reference of subscription payments you make to RescueApps. Subscriptions are paid outside the app; no card details are collected in the app.
  • Notification tokens – for owners and staff who allow notifications, so we can send booking and business updates.
  • Device location – used only when you tap “Use my location” while setting your property's position on the map. Only the coordinates you save are stored.
  • Photos – images you choose from your gallery are uploaded to show your property. The app does not use your camera.
  • App protection – Google Firebase App Check and Play Integrity process device attestation information to protect the service from abuse.

How we use your information

  • To make and manage bookings, orders, quote requests and stays.
  • To share a guest's booking or order details (name, phone number, notes and request details) with the business the guest chose, so it can serve and contact them.
  • To show a business's public details, availability, prices and payment instructions to guests.
  • To let owners and their staff run their business, and to manage subscriptions.
  • To send notifications about bookings, orders and business activity.
  • To keep the service secure and prevent abuse.

We do not sell your data. Rescue has no advertising and no third-party analytics or tracking.

Businesses and the guest data they receive

When you book with a business on Rescue, that business receives your booking details and uses them to serve you. Each business is responsible for how it uses and keeps your details, under the data protection laws of its own country. If you have a question about how a business handles your data, you can contact it directly or ask us to help.

Payments

Rescue does not take guest payments and never handles card details. Guests pay the business directly, using the methods that business offers, such as local mobile money or bank transfer. If you enter a payment reference in the app, it is stored so the business can match your payment.

Where your data is processed

Your data is stored and processed on Google Cloud / Firebase, which acts as our service provider. Our database is located in London, United Kingdom (Google Cloud region europe-west2). Some processing also takes place in the United States (us-central1). This means your data is stored and processed outside Malawi and outside most of the countries where Rescue is used. Data is encrypted in transit. By using Rescue, your data is transferred to and processed in these locations.

How long we keep it

We keep account information while the account exists. Booking, order and business records are kept as long as the business needs them for its records. When a guest deletes their account, their personal details are removed from these records as described below.

Deleting your account and data

Guests: in the Rescue app, Home → More → Delete my account (or from My Bookings). Without the app, see how to request deletion or email us.

  • Deleted: your Google sign-in account with Rescue, notification tokens and related counters.
  • Anonymised: your past bookings and orders stay with the business for its accounts, but your name, phone number, email, notes, payment references and account link are removed.
  • Kept: if a business has blocked an account for misuse, a record of the anonymous account ID is kept to stop the block being bypassed. Internal IDs inside some record references may remain but no longer identify you.

If you have an upcoming confirmed or paid booking, you will be asked to cancel it (under the business's normal rules) before deleting your account.

Business owners: in the Rescue Business app, use Delete account from the dashboard menu. Your sign-in and owner profile are deleted. You must first close any active property. Closed properties and their business records (such as staff, reservations, shifts and sales) are kept so they can be recovered and so guests keep their booking history, until you ask us to erase them. To have your business data erased, email us from your account's email address and we will erase it within 30 days.

Staff: your owner can remove you from the team, which deletes your PIN and signs you out. Your name may remain on past business records such as shifts and sales. To ask about your data, contact the business owner or email us.

Email requests are completed within 30 days.

Your rights

Depending on the law where you live, you may have the right to ask for a copy of your personal data, to correct it, to delete it, or to object to how it is used. Email support@rescueapps.com and we will respond within 30 days. You can also complain to the data protection authority in your country. You can turn off notifications and location access in your phone settings at any time.

Children

Rescue and Rescue Business are intended for adults aged 18 and over, and we do not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page. Significant changes will be announced in the apps.
